How to Use DraftDiff

DraftDiff helps you make mathematically sound champion picks by analyzing team compositions.

  • Select a Role: Click an empty slot on your team's side to see recommendations for that role.
  • Pick Champions: Click a champion from the grid to place them in the selected slot. You can also click a champion first, then a slot.
  • Swap Sides: Use the "Swap Sides" button to indicate which side is yours (Blue or Red).
  • Analyze Drafts: Use the "Draft Analysis" tab to see a detailed breakdown of lane matchups and team synergies.
  • Get the Desktop App: For live tracking that automatically syncs with the League client during champion select, download the desktop version.

Understanding the Math (Delta 2)

What is Delta 2?
Delta 2 (or Normalized Delta) is the purest isolation of a specific champion-to-champion interaction. It removes all outside noise to answer one question: "How much does picking Champion A into Champion B change the outcome of the game, purely because of how their kits interact?"

It adjusts for three things to ensure accuracy:

  1. Champion A's base win rate (How strong your champion is overall).
  2. Champion B's base win rate (How strong the other champion is overall).
  3. The average win rate of the tier (e.g., Emerald+ average win rate is usually around 51-52%, not 50%).

What a +3.26 value literally means:
Based on how strong Champion A is right now, and how strong Champion B is right now, Champion A should beat Champion B X% of the time. However, Champion A actually beats Champion B X + 3.26% of the time. That +3.26 advantage is strictly generated by the specific dynamic between the two champions.

Trusting the Data (Min Games):
Because Delta 2 is trustworthy only when the sample size is high enough, the Min Games filter is the most important setting. A matchup with 150 games might show a Delta of +8.00. This is statistical noise, not a true counter. If you set "Min Games" to a reasonable threshold (e.g., 300), you can trust that the math is entirely objective and stripped of meta-bias.
