How to Use DraftDiff

DraftDiff helps you make better champion picks by analyzing team compositions in real-time.

  • Select a Role: Click an empty slot on your team's side to see recommendations for that role.
  • Pick Champions: Click a champion from the grid to place them in the selected slot. You can also click a champion first, then a slot.
  • Live Tracking: Click "Start Live Tracking" to automatically sync with the League of Legends client during champion select.
  • Analyze Drafts: Use the "Draft Analysis" tab to see a detailed breakdown of lane matchups and team synergies.

How It Works

The app calculates an "Expected Win Rate" for every possible pick. This is based on a champion's base strength, their synergy with your teammates, and their matchups against your enemies. The core logic uses a sophisticated Elo-based rating system to determine how much a champion is expected to over- or under-perform in any given draft.

All data is scraped from LoLalytics and updated twice daily. The data is fetched directly from a cloud storage bucket, ensuring speed and reliability.

Default Settings

For first-time users, the app starts with these recommended filter settings:

  • Min Base WR: 45%
  • Min Role Freq: 5%
  • Min Games: 35
  • Consider Hovers: Ticked
  • Assume Perfect Balance: Ticked
