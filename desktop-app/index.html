Get started with DraftDiff

Find picks that fit your team and the opponents. Use it for your own pick, suggest champions to a teammate, or explore a draft together.

  1. Choose your data. Select a rank and either the current patch or the last 30 days.
  2. Bring in the draft. Press Start Live Tracking during champion select, or enter picks manually. Your live team is on the left. Live tracking sees the champions, not their actual roles. It estimates roles for both teams using role frequency. If it gets a role wrong, move the champion to the correct slot and use the role lock to keep it there.
  3. Choose a role to help. Press SUGGEST on any role, on either team, to see recommendations from that team's perspective.
  4. Find and share your picks. Use filters, search, or role favorites to narrow the list. COPY TOP 10 copies the first ten available suggestions in the displayed order for you to paste into chat.

Click a recommendation to put it in the draft. Click two role slots to swap their contents, or right-click a filled slot to clear it. Right-click a champion in the grid to set role favorites. Use Draft Analysis to compare the teams.

Learn more about the numbers

Delta 2: a matchup's contribution

LoLalytics defines Delta 2 as the matchup win rate minus the normalised champion win rate in that matchup. Positive values mean a champion wins more often than the normalised win rates suggest; negative values mean less often.

The values are directional: Viktor into Syndra and Syndra into Viktor are separate statistics, and need not be exact opposites. DraftDiff keeps both values from the source data.

Reading the columns

Ally Δ sums interactions with teammates. Enemy Δ sums interactions with opponents. Lane Δ isolates the same-role opponent and is also included in Enemy Δ. Total Δ is Ally Δ + Enemy Δ.

A single interaction's Δ is shown in percentage points. Total Δ is a comparison score, not a predicted match win rate. Your experience on a champion and the way the game is played still matter.

Sample sizes and filters

Min Games applies to each individual interaction. If it has too few games, its contribution is treated as zero. Zero can therefore mean excluded or missing data, as well as a neutral interaction. A higher threshold reduces the influence of small samples; it does not guarantee accuracy.

Base WR is the champion's overall win rate in the selected role and dataset. Role Freq is the share of that champion's appearances in that role. Their minimum filters hide recommendations below your thresholds.

A familiar counterpick reference

Expand High Delta 2 reference to see both directional values for Sylas MID versus Malphite MID from your selected dataset, even when you are suggesting another role. Your choice to expand or collapse it is remembered. It gives context for a strong counterpick, rather than claiming to be the largest value in every dataset. Its raw values remain visible below Min Games, with a low-sample warning.
