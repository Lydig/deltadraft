Two methods for recommending:

The Old Method was like a race where everyone starts at the same line, even if some runners are Olympic athletes and others are amateurs. It only measured the finish time.

The New Method is like a handicapped race. The Olympic athletes have to start further back, and the amateurs get a head start. Now, if an amateur finishes with a decent time, we know they performed exceptionally well for their skill level.







DeltaDraft / DraftDiff

DD is first and foremost meant to be a drafting tool that helps players find new picks or just help them choose the best pick in their given champion pool. At the moment it is based on 50 million games from 3 different recent patch 14's, where it calculates a champion/role's base winrate, and sees how that winrate changes against every single enemy champion, and with every single friendly champion. It then accumulates these "delta's" for every possible pick in the role you are drafting for, and recommends you the pick with the highest accumulated delta.

I was inspired to make this tool because I personally like to vary which champions I play, but I find it hard to justify playing a champion that I'm not very practiced on, because I have no idea if it's a bad pick because of my lack of experience with the pick. With this tool I have already discovered numerous super interesting picks for myself to try in various roles - for example Ivern top, Vex top, Sylas support, Mordekaiser jungle, Zac mid, and many more.

Right now I have confidence in the tool working fine on the data I have, but I would like to move on and future proof the tool by being able to collect data as patches roll out, with new champions and the like. The data I have right now I have gotten from another developer making a similar tool, who decided to kind of "pass on the torch" to others who had a passion for this kind of project. I need to be able to collect more data than the developer API allows.
